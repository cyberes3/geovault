# Data attribution

The areas server uses the following external data sources.

## Oceans

Used for: main ocean names.

> Flanders Marine Institute (2021). Global Oceans and Seas, version 1. Available online
> at https://www.marineregions.org/. https://doi.org/10.14284/542

## Ocean regions

Used for: marine geography sub-regions.

> Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.

## Administrative and geographic data

Used for: administrative boundaries, water bodies, protected areas, and place names.

> © OpenStreetMap contributors. Data available under the Open Database License: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

## Ski resorts

Used for: ski area lookups.

> © OpenStreetMap contributors and OpenSkiMap.org.
